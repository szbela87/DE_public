# Differential Equations (MSc)

Public course materials for the MSc Differential Equations course.

## Course materials

| Lesson | Week | Topic | Materials |
|--------|------|-------|-----------|
| 1 | 1 | First-order ODEs I. Fundamentals and classification of differential equations. First-order ODEs, IVPs, existence and uniqueness (Peano, Picard-Lindelof), and Picard iteration. | [l1p1.pdf](l1p1.pdf) |
| 2 | 2 | First-order ODEs II. Well-posedness (Hadamard), explicit and implicit Euler methods, direction fields, and Taylor series methods. | [l2p2.pdf](l2p2.pdf), [l2p2.ipynb](l2p2.ipynb) |
| 3 | 3 | Second-order linear ODEs. Characteristic equations, variation of parameters, Wronskian, Euler equations, singular points, Taylor series solutions, and boundary value problems. | [l3p3.pdf](l3p3.pdf), [l3p3.ipynb](l3p3.ipynb) |
| 4-5 | 4-5 | Systems of differential equations I. Standard form, linearity, IVPs, existence and uniqueness, phase space, qualitative analysis, equilibria, stability, and phase portraits. | [l4p4.pdf](l4p4.pdf), [l4p4.ipynb](l4p4.ipynb) |
| 6-8 | 6-8 | Linear ODE systems and the matrix exponential. Phase portraits, autonomous and nonlinear systems, linearisation, nullclines, Lyapunov functions, LaSalle's principle, limit cycles, and Poincare-Bendixson theory. | [l67p67_new.pdf](l67p67_new.pdf), [lp67.ipynb](lp67.ipynb) |
| 10 | 10 | Vector calculus I-II. Vector fields, gradient and conservative fields, line integrals, Green's theorem, divergence and curl, surface integrals, Stokes' theorem, and the divergence theorem. | [l10p10.pdf](l10p10.pdf) |
| 12 | 12 | Partial differential equations I-III. Classification, first-order PDEs, the method of characteristics, second-order PDEs, initial and boundary conditions, heat, wave, and Laplace equations. | [l12p12.pdf](l12p12.pdf) |
| 13 | 13 | Introduction to the calculus of variations. Functionals, extrema, first variation, the fundamental lemma, Euler-Lagrange equations, and constrained variational problems. | [lp13.pdf](lp13.pdf) |
